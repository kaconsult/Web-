About K+A Consulting

K+A Consulting is an independent advisory firm in Tbilisi. We help investors, family offices and companies raise and deploy capital, enter and exit markets, and get banking and regulatory matters done in Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan and Kazakhstan.

How we work

  1. First call: your goals, the market and the deal, in confidence.
  2. NDA, signed before any detailed discussion.
  3. Mandate: a retainer plus a success fee; most mandates start at USD 500,000.
  4. Execution and closing, with banks, lawyers and counterparties in each country.

Principles

  • Client money never passes through our accounts. Where needed, funds are held in bank or notary escrow.
  • Every engagement is subject to KYC and AML checks, and we work only with documented sources of funds.
  • We share your information with counterparties only with your consent.
  • When a standard task is better handled by a specialist firm, we say so and introduce you.

Track record

Selected mandates, anonymised: the acquisition of an 80-room hotel in Tbilisi; a EUR 15m UK and EU debt refinancing for a client from Kazakhstan; a USD 4m bank credit line for a commodity trader operating from Armenia and Dubai; USD 1.5m raised from an EU investor for serviced apartments in Tbilisi.

Privacy policy

Who we are

K+A Consulting LLC (registration no. 402235684), Tbilisi, Georgia, is responsible for personal data collected through this website. Contact: at@kaconsulting.ge.

What we collect

The details you send us through the enquiry form, email, phone, WhatsApp or Telegram. This is usually your name, contact details and information about your enquiry. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The site remembers your language choice in your own browser only.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to prepare and carry out an engagement, and to meet our legal obligations, including KYC and anti-money-laundering checks.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the website and our communications (hosting, email, form handling and Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads). With your consent only, counterparties we introduce you to. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for these purposes, or longer where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, and the GDPR where it applies, you can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it or delete it, or object to its use. Write to at@kaconsulting.ge. You can also complain to Georgia's Personal Data Protection Service.
