Buying and investing in real estate in Georgia

Foreigners can buy Georgian residential and commercial property on the same terms as locals, registration takes days, and there is no transfer tax. The risks are in the details: title, land category and the quality of the developer.

Can foreigners buy property in Georgia?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can buy non-agricultural property, including apartments, houses, hotels, offices and warehouses, without restriction. Agricultural land is different: it can generally be owned only by Georgian citizens and Georgian-controlled companies, with narrow exceptions.

How a purchase works

  1. Agree the price and terms, usually with a deposit
  2. Carry out due diligence: the public registry extract (owner, mortgages and other encumbrances, cadastral data) and the land category
  3. For off-plan purchases, confirm the developer's title to the land and its construction permits
  4. Sign the sale agreement at the Public Registry or before a notary
  5. Registration: 4 working days as standard, or 1 day expedited

Residence permit through property

Since 1 March 2026, buying non-agricultural property worth at least USD 150,000 (previously USD 100,000) can qualify the owner for a temporary residence permit. The permit is issued for one year and can be renewed while the property is held.

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy land in Georgia?

Foreigners can buy non-agricultural land freely. Agricultural land is restricted to Georgian citizens and Georgian-controlled companies, with narrow exceptions.

Do I need to be in Georgia to buy property?

No. The purchase can be completed through a representative under a notarised power of attorney.

What taxes do I pay when buying property in Georgia?

There is no transfer tax or stamp duty. Ongoing taxes depend on use: 5% on residential rental income and an annual property tax that depends on family income.

How much do I need to invest for a residence permit?

Since 1 March 2026, at least USD 150,000 in non-agricultural property.
