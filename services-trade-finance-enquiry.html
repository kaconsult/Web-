What happens next

  • Within two business days we tell you whether the trade fits and what the lender will want to see.
  • Then a short call to go through the deal, the counterparties and the payment flow.
  • Then the credit package, which we prepare with you so that the committee can decide without follow-up requests.

Pre-export financing pays your supplier before shipment and is repaid from the buyer's payment. If your need is to be paid earlier on invoices you have already issued, that is receivables finance, which we arrange through different partners; the form will tell us which one you need.
