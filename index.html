Markets

Our markets and the Middle Corridor

The Trans-Caspian International Transport Route, known as the Middle Corridor, links China and Central Asia with Europe through Kazakhstan, the Caspian Sea, Azerbaijan and Georgia, bypassing Russia. Freight on the route has grown sharply since 2022, and with it demand for trade finance, logistics assets and warehousing. We work in all three countries it crosses between Central Asia and the Black Sea, and in neighbouring Armenia.

GE

Georgia

Our home market. Simple company registration, a 15% corporate tax charged only on distributed profits, and banks used to working with foreign clients. The Black Sea ports of Poti and Batumi connect the region to Europe.

AM

Armenia

A fast-growing economy with a strong IT sector and active regional trade. Banks are well capitalised and open to trade and project finance, and Yerevan's prime property market has been active in recent years.

AZ

Azerbaijan

Energy is still the backbone of the economy, while the state invests in non-oil sectors, logistics and the Alat Free Economic Zone beside the Port of Baku. Relationships and a local presence matter here more than anywhere.

KZ

Kazakhstan

Central Asia's largest economy and the eastern gateway of the Middle Corridor. The Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) operates under English common law, which makes deals easier to structure for foreign investors.

FAQ

Common questions

If your question is not here, ask us directly.

How do you charge?

We usually work on a retainer, paid monthly or upfront, plus a success fee paid at closing. Both are agreed in writing in the mandate before work starts.

Is there a minimum deal size?

Most of our mandates start at USD 500,000. For smaller matters, ask us anyway; if we are not the right fit, we will tell you.

Do you hold client money?

Client money never passes through our own accounts. Where a transaction needs it, funds are held in escrow with a bank or notary until the agreed conditions are met.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Georgian, Russian and French.

How do you keep matters confidential?

We sign an NDA before any names or documents are exchanged, and introduce parties to each other only with the consent of both sides.

Do you work outside these four countries?

Our focus is Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan and Kazakhstan, but mandates often involve the UK, the EU and the UAE, as the examples in our track record show.
